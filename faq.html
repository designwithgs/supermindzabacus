Frequently asked questions

What are the prerequisites from the child?

Child should have basic understanding of numbers and is able to read and write numbers.

When is the best time to start?

We recommend that the child is around 6 years old so that he/she has developed basic understanding of numbers.

Will it interfere with school studies?

The course is paced so that it will not interfere with your child's school studies and will only add benefits with time.

Can we have a trial class?

Yes, you can enrol for a free trial class and we are sure that your child will love it.

What if the child missed one session?

We can facilitate extra time in the next session to cover the missed topics.

How can I withdraw from the course?

In case, you want to discontinue, you have to give two weeks of advance notice.
