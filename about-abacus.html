ABACUS HISTORY

The abacus was invented as early as 1220 AD in China to help people with large number calculation, and was known by various names like SUANPAN, SOROBAN OR ZHUSUAN and later on modernised by the Japanese.

The abacus with its beads strung on parallel wires led the Arabs to positional numeration and the concept of zero many centuries before the rest of the world.

LEARNING MATHS SHOULD BE FUN

Abacus programme is to create awareness of numbers and instill the love for maths among kids.

By visualising the tool kids can significantly improve their concentration skills and expand their memory.

BENEFITS OF LIFETIME WITH ABACUS

  • Greater concentration and focus.
  • Faster and accurate arithmetic calculations.
  • Ability to visualise.
  • Memory retention and recall.
  • Improved listening, reading and writing skills.
  • Higher self confidence.

PROGRAMME STRUCTURE

  • The course is designed for children in the age group of 6-10 years.
  • There are a total of 8 levels and every level is of 12 weeks duration.
  • For younger students an additional basic level can be organised.
  • Small batch size for more personalised attention to each and every student.
  • Two hours class per week which will be customised according to the student's learning ability..
  • Homework will be given after every class.
  • Students would be assessed after every level for the promotion to the next level.
  • Course material will be provided at the start of every level.
  • Completion certificates will be given after the end of every level.
